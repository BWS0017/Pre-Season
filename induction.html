HOW THE PROGRAMME WORKS
▶
1
The programme runs for 12 weeks across 6 circuits plus 1 bonus circuit. Each circuit covers a different leadership topic.
2
Each circuit has 4 laps: The Starting Grid, The Technical Turn, The Practice Lap, and The Acceleration Lap.
3
A new circuit unlocks every two weeks on Monday morning. Complete each circuit to unlock the next.
4
Weekly commitment is approximately 2 hours — flexible and self-paced within each two-week window.
✅
ASSESSMENT AND EVIDENCE
▶
1
Pit Stop Quiz — 8 knowledge questions per circuit. Pass mark is 75% (6/8). Unlimited retakes.
2
Race Day Challenge — apply your learning to a real person or situation. 250–350 words. Assessed by your Principal Director.
3
F1 Reflection Log — three structured prompts every circuit. This is your ILM evidence. Be honest and specific.
🤖
USING AI IN THIS PROGRAMME
▶
1
Every circuit includes the Pit Wall Coach — a live AI coaching bot to accelerate your thinking.
2
AI is a thinking partner, not an answer machine. Adapt everything to your real context.
3
Never use AI to write your assessment. Your Reflection Log and Race Day Challenge must be in your own words.
🏆
BADGES, LEADERBOARD AND CERTIFICATION
▶
1
You earn a circuit badge when you pass the quiz and submit the Race Day Challenge.
2
The Podium Leaderboard tracks all drivers. Points are earned for circuits, reflections, quizzes, and challenges.
3
Completing the full programme earns your ILM Assured Digital Credential — internationally recognised and shareable on LinkedIn.
🤝
COMMUNITY AND LIVE SESSIONS
▶
1
Every Thursday — a live webinar via Microsoft Teams, approximately 60 minutes.
2
Every Friday — a Pit Stop Reflection community post sharing one insight from your week.
3
The community is a safe space. Support your fellow drivers. What is shared here stays here.
QUICK ANSWERS
What if I miss a week?▶
Each circuit is open for two weeks. If you fall behind, contact your Race Director. Do not disappear — ask for help.
Do I need to be online at set times?▶
Most of the programme is self-paced and async. The only fixed time is the Thursday live webinar. Everything else can be done when it suits you within the two-week window.
What is ILM Assured?▶
ILM stands for Institute of Leadership and Management. ILM Assured means this programme has been reviewed and approved as meeting their leadership development standards. Your certificate is internationally recognised.
What language is the programme in?▶
All content is in English. If anything is difficult to understand, let your Principal Director know. We want everyone to succeed.
How do I get my ILM certificate?▶
You receive your ILM Assured Digital Credential once you complete all 6 circuits, pass all quizzes, and submit all Race Day Challenges and Reflective Accounts. Your Principal Director confirms completion.
